About Us

Strengthening Harvests Across Indonesia

Bene Pyto Tanijaya is an Indonesian producer of chemical fertilizers and crop nutrition solutions. Led by our flagship product, BenSil+, we help farmers strengthen crops, resist disease, and increase yields. Our mission is to build a more productive, sustainable, and profitable future for agriculture.
