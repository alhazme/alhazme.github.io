A picture for every little plan

01 / Picture cards

Familiar pictures.
A clearer day.

From getting dressed to winding down, Dino and Dina make everyday activities easy to recognise. Tap a card to hear its name in English or Bahasa Indonesia.

Start with 10 free cards. Langkah Pro opens all 60, plus your own photos and voice recordings.

Meet the activity cards
At their own pace

02 / A gentle timer

A little time.
Without the rush.

A simple ring shows how much time is left. When it ends, the card waits. Your child taps Done when they are ready to move on.

No flashing. No sudden switch. And if your day does not need a timer, just leave it off.

Optional on either or both activities
One board. One star.

03 / Little wins

Every little step
deserves a hooray.

Finish both activities and a little celebration is waiting. One star for each completed board, shared with a familiar friend.

The stars your child has earned are theirs to keep, including on days when a board is left unfinished.

Progress to keep, at their own pace

04 / Made for both of you

Your helping hand.
Their little world.

In Parent Mode, choose cards, set timers and name the board. In Child Mode, little hands get a big picture and one clear action.

Settings and purchases stay behind a grown-up question. Your child can focus on the step in front of them.
