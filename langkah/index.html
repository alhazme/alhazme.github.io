First brush teeth. Then play.

In the Langkah app, you choose two picture cards for one board: the first activity and the one that follows. Your child can see the order before starting.

Choose pictures of Dino and Dina for everyday activities. Your child can tap a card to hear its name in English or Bahasa Indonesia.

The 10 cards below are free. Langkah Pro unlocks all 60 cards, plus your own photos and voice recordings.

Show your child how much time is left.

Once the activity order is clear, you can add a timer to either or both cards. The timer ring shows the time remaining, for example while your child plays.

When time is up, the card waits for your child to tap Done. Your child can finish the activity before moving on. You can also use the board without a timer.

The ring turns peach and displays Time's up, without red or flashing.

Two steps finished, one star earned.

Your child earns one star when both activities are finished. Dino or Dina celebrates with them on screen.

Your child keeps the stars they have earned, including on days when they leave a board unfinished.

You prepare the board. Your child follows one step at a time.

Parent Mode

  • Pick two cards for your child's routine and name the board.
  • Add a timer if needed, or leave it off.
  • Choose Dino or Dina, then open the board to start Child Mode.

Child Mode

  • Your child sees the current activity card and a hint of what comes next.
  • After the first activity, your child taps Done to open the second card.
  • After the second activity, your child taps Done to receive one star.

To return to settings, a grown-up taps the lock icon and answers one question.
