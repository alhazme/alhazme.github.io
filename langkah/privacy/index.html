Privacy policy

Langkah: First Then, for iPhone and iPad. Last updated 15 November 2026.

The short version

  • Boards, photos and voice recordings you add stay on your device.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends crash reports and device details to Google so we can find and fix app crashes.
  • The app has no advertising or Google Analytics.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple.

Who we are

Langkah: First Then ("Langkah", "the app") is made by Mochamad Fariz Al Hazmi, an individual developer in Indonesia ("we"). This policy covers the app and this website. If anything here is unclear, write to us at the address in Contact.

What stays on your device

You can use Langkah without telling us anything. What you add is saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Your child's name, if you choose to enter one, and the buddy you pick (Dino or Dina).
  • Boards, activity cards, timers and stars.
  • Photos you choose for your own cards.
  • Voice recordings you make for your own cards.
  • Your settings.

We cannot see, copy or recover this content. The app does not send it to us or to Google. The app does not ask for gender, a diagnosis, or any health information.

Device backups

If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or to a computer, Apple includes the app's data in that backup, as it does for other apps. The backup belongs to your Apple Account and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it.

What the app asks permission for

  • Microphone. Only when you record your own voice for a card. Recordings stay on your device.
  • Photos. You pick a photo with Apple's photo picker. The app receives only the photo you choose and cannot browse your library.
  • Notifications. To tell you when a timer ends while the app is in the background. These are created on your device. We send no push notifications.

Read aloud uses the voices built into your device.

Purchases

Langkah Pro is a one-time purchase through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never see your card details, your name or your email address. To check that Pro is unlocked, the app asks the App Store on your device. Apple gives us sales totals, not who bought.

Crash reports

Langkah uses Firebase Crashlytics, a crash-reporting service provided by Google. When the app crashes, Crashlytics can send Google a crash trace, relevant app state, device and operating system details, the app version, and installation identifiers. Google processes these details to group crash reports and help us diagnose and fix problems. Crash reports may be processed and stored in countries where Google operates. Google documents a 90-day retention period for crash traces and associated identifiers. Read how Firebase handles data.

We do not include your child's name, photos, voice recordings, board contents or free-text identifiers in crash reports. We do not use Google Analytics, so Analytics breadcrumb logs are not included. Crash reporting is separate from Apple's optional diagnostics sharing setting. We do not have an in-app switch to turn Crashlytics collection off.

Diagnostics shared by Apple

Separately, if you have turned on "Share With App Developers" in your device settings, Apple may show us diagnostics and usage statistics. You can turn this off in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Langkah is set up by a parent or carer and used together with a child. It does not collect profile or routine details from children or adults. Crash reports described above may be sent when the app crashes. In Child Mode your child sees only the board, with no advertising and no links out of the app. Leaving Child Mode takes a grown-up answering a question. Settings and the purchase screen are in Parent Mode only.

Deleting your data

You can delete a card, a board or a child profile inside the app at any time. Deleting the app from your device removes everything it stored. Copies inside a device backup are managed in your Apple Account settings.

Your rights

Privacy laws give you the right to see, correct and delete personal data that a company holds about you. We do not receive your boards, photos or recordings from the app. Crash reports are processed by Google as described above. If you have emailed us and want that email deleted, tell us and we will delete it. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

This website

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking scripts. The app uses Firebase Crashlytics; this does not add tracking scripts to the website. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when you load a page. See the GitHub privacy statement.

If you email us

We receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and we do not add you to any list. Your email stays in our mailbox so we can follow up, until you ask us to delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you.
